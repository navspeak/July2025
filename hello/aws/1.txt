- I designed an event-driven architecture where incoming data—starting from S3—triggered a flexible pipeline.
 - A Lambda dispatcher routed jobs:
     - smaller tasks ran directly in Lambda,
     - medium tasks spun up Fargate,
     - and complex workflows triggered MWAA (Airflow).
  - From Airflow, we orchestrated PySpark jobs in Databricks.
  - While the team handled deep Databricks work, I ensured the entire system was scalable and event-driven end to end.
