management:
  endpoints:
    web:
      exposure:
        include: health,info,metrics
  endpoint:
    health:
      show-details: always



1. Health endpoint (includes Resilience4j circuit breaker health indicators)
        GET /actuator/health
    You’ll see something like:
        circuit breaker state (CLOSED / OPEN / HALF_OPEN)
        failure rate info (depends on version/config)

2. Metrics endpoint (raw counters/timers)
        GET /actuator/metrics

3.You can also query a specific metric like:
    GET /actuator/metrics/resilience4j.circuitbreaker.calls

(Exact metric names can vary a bit by version, but resilience4j.circuitbreaker.* is the pattern.)
