## Out of box
- Just by including spring security in pom gives protects all your endpoint with both formLogin() and httpBasic().
- It uses "Content Negotiation" to decide how to challenge the requester:
  -  It is Form-Based for Browsers.If you open your endpoint in Chrome or Firefox, the browser sends a header: Accept: text/html.
    - Spring's Response: "Oh, you're a human with a browser. Here is a pretty (well, 'Spring-pretty') login page."
      - Result: You get redirected to /login. 
  - It is Basic Auth for Tools (like HTTPie/curl). If you use curl, it typically sends Accept: */* or application/json.
    - Spring's Response: "You look like a command-line tool or an API client. I won't send you HTML; 
    - I'll just send a 401 Unauthorized status with a WWW-Authenticate: Basic header."
      - Result: Your terminal won't show a login page; it will just fail until you provide credentials.
- User: user, password: generated
